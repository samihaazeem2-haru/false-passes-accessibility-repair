## Caveats

- The sample covers only checker-passing AccessGuru-final repairs, so the false-pass rate is P(not truly fixed | checker pass) for that method.
- Round-2 cases exclude every round-1 case. Because round 1's cases were themselves random, round 2 is still a random sample of cases, but the two rounds together are not one simple random sample. That is one more reason the combined estimate is secondary.
- A case is one dataset row (one violation). Several cases can come from the same web page, so some round-2 items share a page with round-1 items (count above), and the bootstrap, which resamples cases, does not model page-level correlation.
- The labellers are the author (A) and a classmate (B), not independent experts.
- The judges are free-tier models, each from the same family as one repairer; judge prompts are unchanged from round 1 (rubric v1 wording).
